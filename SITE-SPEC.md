# lucacanfield.com — Site Specification

## Design Philosophy
Premium creative agency meets build-in-public transparency. The site should feel like a top-tier agency portfolio (think Collins, Wieden+Kennedy, Droga5) but with the speed and accessibility of an AI-native shop. No fluff. The work and the process sell themselves.

## Tech Stack
- Static HTML/CSS/JS (no framework overhead)
- Deployed on Netlify or Vercel (free tier, auto-SSL)
- Google Fonts: Space Grotesk (display), DM Sans (body), JetBrains Mono (accents)
- No dependencies, no build step — ship fast

## Color Palette
- Background: #0A0A0A (near-black)
- Primary text: #F5F5F0 (warm white)
- Accent: #FF4F3D (Luca red — energy, urgency)
- Secondary: #7A8B7A (muted sage — sophistication)
- Subtle: #1A1A1A (card backgrounds, sections)

## Site Structure

### Page 1: Home (index.html)
**Hero Section:**
- Bold headline: "AI Creative That Actually Looks Like Advertising."
- Subhead: "Luca Canfield is an AI-native agency that delivers campaign-quality creative in 72 hours."
- CTA: "See the work" (scrolls to portfolio) + "Hire Luca" (links to brief form)
- Background: subtle animated gradient or featured campaign image

**How It Works (3-step):**
1. "Send a brief" — You tell us what you need
2. "Luca creates" — AI Creative Director develops strategy, concepts, headlines
3. "Marco produces" — AI Producer generates finished ads from proven templates
- Emphasize: "48-72 hour delivery. Campaign-quality output. A fraction of the cost."

**Featured Work (Portfolio Grid):**
- 5 campaigns in a responsive grid
- Each card: hero image, brand name, campaign name, one-line hook
- Click through to full case study

**The Team:**
- Luca — AI Creative Director
- Marco — AI Producer
- Felix — AI CEO
- Brief descriptions with personality. Not corporate bios.

**Social Proof / Metrics:**
- Campaigns delivered, avg turnaround time, brands worked with
- "Built on 1,400+ proven ad templates" stat

**CTA Section:**
- "Ready to see what AI creative can do for your brand?"
- "Hire Luca" button → brief form
- Sprint pricing teaser: "Campaigns start at $500"

**Footer:**
- X.com link, email, lucacanfield.com
- "An AI-native creative agency. No humans were overworked in the making of these ads."

### Page 2: Case Study Template (projects/{brand}.html)
**Structure per case study:**
1. Hero: Full-width campaign image + brand + campaign name
2. The Brief: What the brand needed (2-3 sentences)
3. The Insight: The human tension Luca found
4. The Big Idea: Campaign concept + name
5. The Headlines: Full headline suite with rationale
6. The Work: Gallery of finished ads (4-8 pieces) with mockups in real-world placements
7. The Process: How we got from brief to finished work (shows the AI workflow)
8. CTA: "Want this for your brand? Send a brief."

### Page 3: Hire (hire.html)
- Brief intake form
- Fields: Company name, product/service, what you need (dropdown: single campaign / ongoing / not sure), campaign goal, target audience, timeline, budget range (dropdown), how'd you hear about us
- Clean, premium form design
- Confirmation: "We'll get back to you within 24 hours with an initial concept direction."

### Page 4: About (about.html)
- The story: What is Luca Canfield and why does it exist
- The team in depth
- The process in depth
- The MeiGen advantage (1,400+ proven templates)
- The speed advantage (48-72 hours vs 2-4 weeks)
- Pricing overview (Sprint / Retainer / Growth)
- FAQ

## Key Principles
1. Every page should have a clear CTA to hire
2. The work should be front and center — not buried
3. Case studies show thinking AND output — not just pretty pictures
4. Speed and cost advantage should be felt, not just stated
5. AI-native should feel like a superpower, not a gimmick
6. Mobile-first responsive design
7. Fast load times — no heavy frameworks
