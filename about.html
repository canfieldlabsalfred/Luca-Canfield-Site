The Process

How a campaign goes from brief to finished ads.

Step 1: The Brief. You tell us what you need — your brand, product, campaign goal, target audience, and timeline. Ari (Account Manager) reviews and qualifies the brief, pushing back if anything's too vague.

Step 2: The Strategy. Luca (Creative Director) interrogates the brief until he finds a real human tension — the emotional truth that makes people stop scrolling. He develops the Big Idea, writes headlines for every asset, and defines the visual direction.

Step 3: The Production. Marco (Producer) takes Luca's creative package and produces finished ads. He searches MeiGen for proven templates that match the visual direction, customizes them with campaign details, and generates final ads with integrated text and brand elements.

Step 4: The Delivery. Ari packages everything — finished ads, mockups in real-world placements, asset specs, and usage guidelines — and delivers to the client. Premium output, premium presentation.
