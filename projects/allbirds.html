Case Study

Allbirds: "WANT / FROM"

Made from trees. Made to be wanted.
Allbirds
Tree Runner
DTC / Sustainability
8 Ads — Social, Display
24 Hours
The Brief

Reposition Allbirds from "that sustainable shoe brand" to a brand people actually desire.

Allbirds had a problem every purpose-driven brand eventually faces: people respected them, but nobody lusted after them. The Tree Runner — made from eucalyptus tree fiber — was a great product trapped in a dutiful brand narrative. The brief was clear: make sustainability desirable, not just admirable.

Target audience: urban professionals 25-40 who wear Nike, New Balance, or Veja. They care about sustainability but won't sacrifice style for it.

The Insight

Desire vs. duty.

"I want to make good choices. But I don't want good-actor trophies. I want to be admired, not applauded."

Luca dug into the real tension: every sustainable choice this audience has ever made came with a tax. The ugly reusable tote. The clunky electric car. The "earth-first" shoes that look like they'd rather be hiking than seen at dinner. They've been forced to choose between being good and being wanted.

The Tree Runner isn't a compromise shoe. It's a shoe that happens to be made from trees — and it should be the most desirable everyday sneaker they've ever seen.

The Big Idea

WANT / FROM

Flip the script on sustainability marketing. Every eco-brand says "buy this because it's better for the world." We said: "buy this because it's better, period. The world just happens to agree with your desire."

This isn't green marketing. This is desire marketing that happens to be green. Lead with want. Let the sustainability be the bonus, not the pitch.

The Headlines

8 concepts. Zero apologies.

  • "The compromise is over." Editorial product shot — high-fashion, not hiking
  • "What your shoes say about you." Split-frame comparison — mysterious, confidential
  • "Should. Want. Same thing now." Brutalist typography — provocative, slightly smug
  • "They'll ask what it is. They won't expect the answer." Candid urban lifestyle — effortless cool
  • "Not for the cause. For the crowd." Texture close-up — tactile, sensory
  • "This is what sustainable looks like when it's not trying." Three-shot product grid — refined gallery aesthetic
  • "They thought comfort was the trade-off." Low-angle hero shot — sneakerhead energy
  • "Your secret weapon." POV feet shot — conspiratorial, golden hour

The Process

Brief to delivery in 24 hours.

Hour 0: Client brief received — product, audience, tone, and campaign goal.

Hours 1-4: Luca (Creative Director) interrogates the brief. Finds the real human tension: desire vs. duty. Develops the Big Idea: "WANT / FROM." Writes 8 headlines with full visual direction per ad.

Hours 4-12: Marco (Producer) takes Luca's creative package, searches MeiGen for proven templates matching each visual direction, and generates 8 finished ads through Nanobanana 2 with integrated typography and brand elements.

Hours 12-24: Ari (Account Manager) packages the deliverables — finished ads, mockups, asset specs — and delivers to the client.

No rounds of revisions that take weeks. No creative blocks. No meetings. Just the work.
